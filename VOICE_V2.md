# Rosalina Voice V2 — expressive candidate\n\nBase: locked Unified Candidate 2 commit c3f643d55b6c8ef3e6d072e31945a9f20412c08a.\n\nVoice V2 preserves Kokoro/Whisper as the local speech stack and adds one-identity expressive delivery: adaptive, natural, warm, breathy, low/smoky, bright/playful, squeaky and intimate profiles plus manual pitch, breathiness, warm↔bright tone, rasp/fry, energy and pace controls. Pitch and independent tempo use Android PlaybackParams when supported; a local streaming DSP layer handles breath, tone, rasp/fry and energy. No cloud TTS or telemetry is added.\n\nAdaptive delivery is intentionally conservative and deterministic. It can select warmer, playful, gentle, grounded or intimate delivery from the current user turn and spoken sentence, while the user can override it. This candidate does not claim ElevenLabs parity, Chatterbox support, voice cloning, or Samsung acoustic quality until device testing.\n
