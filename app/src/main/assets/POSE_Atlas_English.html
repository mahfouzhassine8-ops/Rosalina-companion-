About POSE Atlas

This independent English-language companion helps artists browse the public AI Pose Gallery reference collection.

How to use

Search by English pose name, category or ID. Filter by collection and camera angle, tap to view the full-size image, swipe sideways to change angles, and use the star to keep favorites on this device.

Original source & rights

The original project and translated source data are maintained by Anhao, based on material by Gia (@giaaibusi). The original images and data are not covered by the source-code MIT license. Images are streamed from the original public gallery, not bundled or re-licensed here. Some references depict adult-themed positions; this gallery is intended for adults.

The gallery needs an internet connection to fetch source metadata and images. Any saved favorites and previously loaded metadata are kept locally on your device.
